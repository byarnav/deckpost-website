Features

From deck to carousel
in one click.

Any page. Any format.

Pages, Long Image, Grid or Pictures. PNG, JPG, HEIC or TIFF, at 150, 300 or 600 DPI.

Your brand on every slide.

Your logo, two colours and your @handle on every slide. Save one brand per client and switch in one click.

Slides people swipe.

Square, Portrait and Story sizes. One gradient flows across every slide, with “Swipe →” and slide numbers like “1 / 5”.

See it before you post.

See your carousel in a phone frame and swipe through the slides. The Story safe zone keeps text clear of the top and bottom bars.

Right-click. Done.

Right-click a PDF in Finder and choose “Convert to Images”. Or use the Shortcuts action for your own automations.

Help

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support@byarnav.com. Please write “DeckPost” in the subject.

Does it work offline?

Yes, fully. DeckPost never connects to the internet. Everything happens on your Mac.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No. There is no account and no subscription. You pay once.

Which formats can it make?

PNG, JPG, HEIC and TIFF, at 150, 300 or 600 DPI.

How do I use it from Finder?

Right-click a PDF in Finder and choose “Convert to Images”. It uses the settings you picked in the app.

Where are my brand logos stored?

Only on your Mac. DeckPost keeps its own copy of each logo, and it never leaves your computer.

Which macOS do I need?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
