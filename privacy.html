Privacy Policy

November 2026

Fully offline

DeckPost works completely offline. It contains no networking code, connects to no servers, and sends nothing over the internet.

No data collection

DeckPost collects no personal data and no usage data. There are no analytics, no crash reports, no tracking, no cookies and no accounts.

Your files stay on your Mac

Your PDFs and the images made from them are processed on your Mac. Results are saved only to the folder you pick.

Brands

The logos, colours and @handles you add in the Brands window are stored only on your Mac.

Sharing

When you use Share, AirDrop, Mail, drag, Copy or a Shortcut, the files go only where you send them. macOS and the app you pick handle that step. DeckPost does not see or keep a copy.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Like any web host, the server may keep basic access logs for security.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@byarnav.com.
